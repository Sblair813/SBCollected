# COPI — Core Onboarding Product Integrity

COPI is a product management case study exploring how organizations can make product information more complete, consistent, and manageable throughout the product lifecycle.

The initial module, **COPI Onboarding**, focuses on the process of receiving product information, evaluating it against applicable requirements, identifying gaps, requesting missing information, and revalidating the product until requirements are satisfied.

## What this case study demonstrates

* Product vision and problem definition
* User and stakeholder analysis
* Workflow design
* Product requirements
* MVP definition and prioritization
* User stories and acceptance criteria
* Product decisions and tradeoffs
* AI opportunities within a broader product workflow

## Product approach

COPI combines structured requirements and validation with AI where it can reduce manual effort.

AI may assist with activities such as interpreting unstructured responses, extracting information from documents, identifying potential inconsistencies, and drafting requests or follow-ups.

The product is designed to keep requirements, decisions, and accountability understandable and traceable rather than relying on AI alone.
