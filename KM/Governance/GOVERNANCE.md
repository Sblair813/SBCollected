# Knowledge Management Governance & Lifecycle Policy

## 1. Document Ownership & Maintenance
- **Single Point of Contact:** Every knowledge asset must list an explicit functional owner.
- **Review Schedule:** Core technical documentation must undergo quarterly verification.
- **Lifecycle States:**
  * `Draft`: In progress, pending review.
  * `Verified`: Reviewed and active.
  * `Deprecated`: Outdated or replaced; retained for historical context only.

## 2. Quality & Verification Standards
- Technical workflows and scripts must include verification steps or sample outputs.
